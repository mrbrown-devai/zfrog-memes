FAQ
Quick answers.

      
Is it really free?
Yes. No purchase, no fee, no connecting your wallet. You only paste your X handle and two public wallet addresses.

      
How are winners picked?
At random, from the entries that pass the check, after entries close on 17 Nov 2026 at 23:59 London time. Entering does not guarantee a prize.

      
What can I win?
$ZFROG, sent to your Solana wallet, and a ZFROG Genesis NFT, sent to your Ethereum wallet.

      
How do I know if I won?
Winners are announced on @FrogMark1 and in the Telegram channel, and prizes go straight to the wallets you entered. We never DM first.

      
Why do you need my X handle and two wallets?
The X handle is how we check the follow, like, quote, comment and sticker tweet. The Solana address receives $ZFROG and the Ethereum address receives the NFT. They are stored privately and only used for this draw.

      
Which wallets should I use?
For Solana, a wallet you control that supports Token-2022 tokens: Phantom, Solflare or Backpack. For Ethereum, any wallet you control, like MetaMask, Rabby or Phantom. Not exchange deposit addresses, which may not credit the prizes.

      
What are the $ZAMA drops?
Every $ZFROG transfer pays a 3% tax. 2.5% of that tax covers network and operating costs, and the other 2.925% of the transfer is swapped to $ZAMA and sent to every wallet holding at least $20 of $ZFROG. The full breakdown is in the rewards guide.

      
Is this run by Zama or Breakpoint?
No. ZFROG is an independent community project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Zama, the Solana Foundation or Breakpoint.

      
$ZFROG is a community memecoin with no intrinsic value and no expectation of financial return. The value of any prize can go up or down, and $ZAMA drops are never guaranteed.
