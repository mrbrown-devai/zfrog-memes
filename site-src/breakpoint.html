FAQ

Quick answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. No purchase, no fee, no connecting your wallet. You only paste your public Solana address.

How much $ZFROG do I get?

A bit over $20 worth of $ZFROG at the price on the day we send, enough to clear the $20 line that makes a wallet eligible for $ZAMA drops. The 3% transfer tax is already counted.

When does the airdrop land?

In one batch after Breakpoint London, once every entry has been checked. The date goes out on @FrogMark1 and the Telegram channel.

Why do you need my X and Telegram names?

To check that the follow, the join and the like are real before anything is sent. They are stored privately and only used for this airdrop.

Which wallet should I use?

Any Solana wallet you control that supports Token-2022 tokens: Phantom, Solflare or Backpack. Not an exchange deposit address, which may not credit the token or earn $ZAMA drops.

What are the $ZAMA drops?

Every $ZFROG transfer pays a 3% tax. 2.5% of that tax covers network and operating costs, and the other 2.925% of the transfer is swapped to $ZAMA and sent to every wallet holding at least $20 of $ZFROG. The full breakdown is in the rewards guide.

Is this run by Zama or Breakpoint?

No. ZFROG is an independent community project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Zama, the Solana Foundation or Breakpoint.

$ZFROG is a community memecoin with no intrinsic value and no expectation of financial return. The value of the airdrop can go up or down, and $ZAMA drops are never guaranteed.
