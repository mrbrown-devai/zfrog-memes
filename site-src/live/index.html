How it works

A simple idea. A little swamp magic.

Every trade feeds the pot. The pot turns into $ZAMA. The $ZAMA lands in holders' wallets on its own.

Hold $ZFROG

Keep at least $20 of $ZFROG in your Solana wallet. That is the only condition to receive drops.

3% fills the pot

A 3% Token-2022 transfer tax is withheld on every $ZFROG transfer. About 2.5% of that covers network and operating costs; the rest goes to the pot.

Dropped in $ZAMA

Once the pot is worth selling, it is swapped to $ZAMA and sent pro rata to every eligible wallet. Nothing to claim.

The real machine

The frog is real hardware.

FROG Mark 1 is the name given to Zama's first FPGA supercomputer: racks of FPGA boards built to run fully homomorphic encryption, which means computing on data without ever decrypting it.

$ZFROG is the community meme that grew around it. The hood hides the face, the mask filters the air, the chip on the chest keeps the secrets.
