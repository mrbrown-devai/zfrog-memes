Shielded by default

A PFP for people who hide things.

Most NFTs are public the second they mint. Genesis frogs are not. Every frog lives 100% onchain: the art and its traits sit in the contract on Ethereum, no IPFS, no server. The traits are encrypted with Zama's fully homomorphic encryption, so the contract can hold them and nobody else can read them.

Minted sealed

Every card is drawn at mint and stored encrypted. Explorers, marketplaces and the team see a sealed frog, nothing more.

Unshield it

Only the holder can decrypt their frog's traits and see it first. Your bag, your eyes, your timing.

Show it, or don't

Publish your frog when you choose and it becomes public for good. Keep it sealed and it stays yours alone.

FAQ

Quick answers.

What is ZFROG Genesis?

The first NFT collection of the Pond: 284 cards on Ethereum, hooded pixel frogs and one-of-ones, fully onchain and minted shielded with Zama's fully homomorphic encryption.

Are the frogs onchain?

Yes, 100%. The art and the traits live in the contract on Ethereum. No IPFS, no external server. As long as Ethereum runs, your frog exists.

What does "minted shielded" mean?

Each frog's traits are encrypted on Ethereum. Nobody can see them, the team included. Only the holder can unshield a frog, see it first, and choose when to show it to the world.

Why does my frog look sealed on a marketplace?

Because it is. Until its holder publishes it, a frog shows as a sealed silhouette everywhere. That is the point.

How is Genesis linked to $ZFROG?

Same Pond, same frog. $ZFROG is our community memecoin on Solana that pays holders in $ZAMA. Genesis is its first art collection on Ethereum. Read more in the rewards guide and the Pond Codex.

No. ZFROG is an independent community project. Genesis uses Zama's technology but is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by Zama. For official Zama information, go to zama.org.
