How the rewards work

$ZFROG is a Solana Token-2022 token with a transfer fee built in. Here is the loop:

  1. 3% is withheld on every transfer. Buys, sells and wallet-to-wallet moves all count. This uses Solana's Token-2022 transfer fee extension.
  2. About 2.5% covers network and operating costs. The rest goes into the reward pot.
  3. stonk.fun swaps the pot into $ZAMA.
  4. $ZAMA is sent automatically, pro rata, to every wallet holding at least $20 of $ZFROG.

No staking. No claim button. No lockup. If your wallet holds the minimum, $ZAMA shows up. More volume in the swamp means a bigger pot. The live total paid to holders is on the homepage counter.

How to buy $ZFROG on Raydium

  1. Get a Solana wallet. Phantom, Solflare or Backpack all work. Write your seed phrase down offline and never share it.
  2. Fund it with SOL. Buy SOL on an exchange and withdraw it to your wallet. Keep a little extra for network fees.
  3. Open Raydium. Type raydium.io yourself or use your wallet's in-app browser. Fake Raydium sites exist.
  4. Connect your wallet.
  5. Paste the contract address CZ4dvNHa15HybGwBMDaqWhtN4qVpHa9sayBBVrfxoH2T into the token field. Raydium does not list Token-2022 tokens by name search.
  6. Accept the Token-2022 notice. Raydium shows a risk prompt for Token-2022 tokens. Confirm it to unlock the pool.
  7. Swap SOL for $ZFROG. The 3% transfer fee comes off what lands in your wallet.
  8. Hold at least $20 worth. You are in the pond.

What to do with your $ZAMA: get fully shielded

Your $ZFROG earns $ZAMA on Solana. That is half the stack. To shield, swap, earn and stake, $ZAMA has to be on Ethereum for now. Five steps, one Ethereum wallet. Each step has a short video on our X series.

  1. Bridge $ZAMA from Solana to Ethereum. Open bridge.zama.org, connect your Solana wallet and your Ethereum wallet, and send your $ZAMA across. Keep some SOL for the Solana side and some ETH for gas on Ethereum. Watch step 1.
  2. Shield (and unshield) $ZAMA. In the Zama App, Shield turns $ZAMA into cZAMA, a confidential balance only you can read. Unshield brings it back to a public balance when you need it. Same wallet, two states. Watch step 2.
  3. Swap cZAMA to cUSDT. Still in the Zama App, swap without leaving the confidential state. Amounts and balances stay encrypted. Private in, private out. Watch step 3.
  4. Earn yield with cUSDT. On app.zama.org/earn, deposit cUSDT and earn yield on Ethereum, with amounts still encrypted. Watch step 4.
  5. Stake $ZAMA. On staking.zama.org, delegate $ZAMA to an operator to help secure the protocol and earn staking rewards. Staking uses regular (non-confidential) $ZAMA, so unshield first if needed. Watch step 5.

Type these addresses yourself and check the domain is zama.org. Yield and staking rewards are set by those protocols, not by ZFROG, and are never guaranteed. Bridging, swaps and deposits each cost network fees.

Risks

Read this before you buy.

  • Memecoins are very risky. The price can go toward zero. Only put in what you can afford to lose.
  • Rewards are not guaranteed. They depend on trading volume, the $ZAMA price and the stonk.fun mechanism working as intended.
  • The 3% fee hits every transfer. Flipping in and out costs you each time.
  • The $20 minimum moves with price. If $ZFROG drops, your bag can fall under the line and stop earning until you top up.
  • Third-party risk. Raydium, stonk.fun and Solana are outside our control.
  • Exchange wallets may not count. Rewards go to onchain holder wallets. Keep your $ZFROG in your own wallet.
  • Scams. We never DM first, never ask for your seed phrase and never ask you to "validate" a wallet.

What is $ZAMA, anyway?

$ZAMA is the token of the Zama Confidential Blockchain Protocol, which uses fully homomorphic encryption to keep balances and transfers private on public chains. It pays protocol fees and secures the network through staking. It has traded since 2 February 2026 and lives on Ethereum, BNB Chain and Solana. New to all this? Start with FHE explained by a frog.
